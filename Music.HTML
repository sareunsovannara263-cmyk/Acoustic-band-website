Acoustic~Band

Music from the heart, played with passion.

We are an acoustic band that loves performing music for everyone. Our goal is to inspire people through songs and create unforgettable memories.

Music has the power to bring people together, and that's what we love to do. While the river flows and the fresh breeze surrounds us, we perform songs that fill every moment with warmth and happiness. Sit back, enjoy the view, and let our melodies become part of your memories.

Our band began on September 11, 2025, with a group of friends who shared a passion for music. What started as simple practice sessions soon grew into something special. We believe that music brings people together, and every performance is our way of sharing joy, creating memories, and connecting with our audience. As we continue this journey, we hope our songs inspire and bring happiness to everyone who listens.

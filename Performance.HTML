Our band began on September 11, 2025, with a group of friends who shared a passion for music. What started as simple practice sessions soon grew into something special. We believe that music brings people together, and every performance is our way of sharing enjoy, And it also our fist show performance.

Our second performance on the boat trip was a memorable experience. As the boat gently sailed along the river, we played our favorite acoustic songs while enjoying the beautiful scenery and cool breeze. Seeing everyone smile, sing along, and enjoy the music made us even more passionate about performing. This special day brought us closer as a band and inspired us to continue sharing our music with others.

Our second performance took place during the Khmer New Year (Songkran) celebration, and it was a day we will always remember. Surrounded by festive decorations, smiling faces, and the joyful spirit of the holiday, we performed our favorite songs with excitement and passion. Seeing people sing, clap, and enjoy the music made the celebration even more special. This performance gave us more confidence and motivated us to continue sharing our music with our community.
